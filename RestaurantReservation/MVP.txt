Restaurant Reservation & Analytics Platform — Full Business Logic & Product Specification
PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a lightweight SaaS platform for restaurants, cafes, and bars in Georgia focused on reservation operations, no-show reduction, table organization, and restaurant analytics.

This platform is NOT:

a POS system
an inventory system
an accounting platform
a food delivery platform
a public reservation marketplace initially

The system is an INTERNAL OPERATIONAL TOOL for restaurants.

Restaurants manually enter reservation data received from:

phone calls
Instagram/Facebook messages
WhatsApp
existing reservation websites
walk-ins

The goal is to simplify reservation operations and provide valuable analytics that restaurants currently lack.

CORE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Restaurants in Georgia often manage reservations through:

notebooks
phone calls
WhatsApp
memory
Excel spreadsheets

This creates:

double bookings
table confusion
no-show losses
poor organization
lack of operational insights
staff conflicts
inability to analyze peak hours and traffic

Existing systems like Fina focus mainly on POS, inventory, and accounting, while reservation management is secondary and often inconvenient.

This platform focuses specifically on:

reservation workflow
operational simplicity
table management
analytics
no-show protection

MOST IMPORTANT BUSINESS LOGIC.
